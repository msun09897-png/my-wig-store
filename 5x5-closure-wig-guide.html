What Is a 5x5 Closure Wig?

A 5x5 closure places a focused lace area at the top of the wig. It offers a straightforward parting zone and can support an easy routine when the cap is specifically constructed for glueless wear.

Updated 2026-08-03

Home · Wig Buying Guide · What Is a 5x5 Closure Wig?

What does 5x5 mean on a wig?

The measurement describes an approximately 5-inch-wide by 5-inch-deep lace section around the top-center of the cap. Hair is ventilated into this lace area so the part can look more scalp-like. The remaining cap uses the construction listed on the individual product page.

5x5 closure vs lace front

ConstructionLace areaRoutine to consider
5x5 closureFocused top-center sectionSimple placement and a defined parting area
13x4 lace frontAcross the front with moderate depthMore front-hairline styling space
13x6 lace frontAcross the front with greater depthDeeper parting flexibility

A larger lace area is not automatically better. Choose the construction that matches how often you change your part, how much lace you want to maintain, and the finished style you want to wear.

Can a 5x5 closure wig be glueless?

Some 5x5 closure wigs are designed for glueless wear, but the closure size alone does not guarantee it. Look for an adjustable band, combs and a cap structure specifically described as glueless. The Bouncy Curl Closure has a glueless wear-and-go cap, an adjustable strap, a natural-black 16-inch curl pattern and 200% density.

Who may prefer a 5x5 closure?

  • You normally wear a middle part or a part that stays within the top lace area.
  • You prefer a focused lace section instead of an ear-to-ear lace front.
  • You want a straightforward everyday placement routine.
  • You are choosing a finished curly or bob style that does not require extensive front-lace styling.

How to care for the closure area

  • Handle the lace gently when putting on or removing the wig.
  • Detangle the hair in sections without pulling against the lace.
  • Keep heavy products away from the parting area to reduce visible buildup.
  • Use a strand test and low heat when styling human hair.
  • Store the wig so the closure is not folded or crushed.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 5x5 closure beginner friendly?

It can be a practical option because the lace area is focused at the top. Ease of wear still depends on cap fit, adjustment and whether the specific wig is built for glueless use.

Can I make a side part with a 5x5 closure?

You can adjust the part within the available lace area. The range is smaller than on a 13x4 or 13x6 lace front.

Does 5x5 describe the wig size?

No. It describes the lace section, not the overall cap circumference. Check the product page for cap-adjustment details.
