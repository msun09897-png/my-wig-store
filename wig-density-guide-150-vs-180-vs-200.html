150% vs 180% vs 200% Wig Density

Wig density describes overall fullness, not individual strand thickness. The same density can also look different in a straight bob, long waves or tight curls.

Updated 2026-08-02

Wig density comparison

DensityGeneral appearanceRoutine to consider
150%Natural, lighter fullnessLess bulk and a streamlined everyday look
180%Fuller appearanceMore volume while remaining versatile
200%Very full appearanceMore hair to detangle, dry and style

What 150% density looks like

At 150%, a wig is designed for a relatively natural level of fullness. It can work well for shoppers who do not want a heavy-looking silhouette. The effect still depends on length and texture: a short 150% bob may look compact, while a longer straight style distributes the hair over more length. The Platinum Bob and Signature Straight both include selected 150% options.

What 180% density looks like

At 180%, the wig has more overall fullness than a comparable 150% version. This can give waves more body or make straight lengths appear denser through the ends. It also means more hair to care for. Several ARELVIENNE styles offer 180% options so you can compare the price and length combinations shown on the product page.

What 200% density looks like

At 200%, the result is intentionally full. Curly texture can make that fullness look even more pronounced because the hair occupies more visual space. Our Bouncy Curl Closure is a fixed 16-inch, 200% density style. Choose it because you want the dense curly effect shown, not simply because the percentage is higher.

Texture and length change the result

Density percentages are most useful when comparing similar constructions. Curls and waves usually appear fuller than straight hair at the same density. Longer hair spreads the same overall fullness over more length, while a blunt bob concentrates the ends into a shorter outline. Product photos, listed texture and length are therefore important alongside the percentage.

Which density should you choose?

  • 150%: choose this if you prefer lighter-looking fullness and a simpler daily routine.
  • 180%: choose this if you want a noticeably fuller shape and are comfortable caring for more hair.
  • 200%: choose this if a deliberately dense finish matches the style you want.

Use density together with the product's lace construction, texture, length and color. Never choose from the percentage alone. You can compare all available combinations in the ARELVIENNE wig collection.

Frequently asked questions

Does higher density mean higher quality?

No. Density describes fullness. Quality, construction, hair handling and correct care are separate considerations.

Will 180% always feel heavy?

Not necessarily. Perceived weight varies with cap size, length, texture and the individual wearer. More density does mean more hair than a comparable 150% style.
