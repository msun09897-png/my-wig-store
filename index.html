Returns & Refunds

Last updated: July 2026

Our 30-Day Return Policy

We want you to love your LUMIÈRE piece. If you are not completely satisfied, you may request a return within 30 days of the delivery date.

Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions:

  • Returned within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date.
  • Unworn, unaltered, and undamaged.
  • In its original packaging with all tags, nets, and accessories included.
  • Free from any styling products, perfume, or odors.

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Any wig or hair extension that has been removed from its sealed packaging and tried on or worn.
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.
  • Custom or bespoke orders.

We take hygiene seriously, and we appreciate your understanding of this policy.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Email us at msun09897@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Our team will review your request and respond within 2 business days with return instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging and ship it to the address we provide.
  4. Share the return tracking number with us via email.

Return Shipping Costs

Customers are responsible for all return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged or we shipped the wrong product. We recommend using a trackable shipping service. LUMIÈRE is not responsible for returned items lost in transit.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you by email. If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Please note that your bank or payment provider may take additional time to post the credit.

Exchanges

We gladly offer exchanges for a different color or length of the same style, subject to availability and the return eligibility requirements above. To request an exchange, please follow the same return initiation process and note your preferred replacement.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please email msun09897@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photographs of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.
