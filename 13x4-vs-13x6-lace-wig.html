13x4 vs 13x6 Lace Wig: What Is the Difference?

Both constructions create a lace-front hairline. The main difference is how far the lace extends from the front hairline toward the crown, which changes the available parting space.

Updated 2026-08-03

13x4 and 13x6 at a glance

The first number describes the approximate width of the lace across the front of the cap. The second number describes its approximate depth at the deepest section. A 13x4 lace front therefore covers a similar width to a 13x6, but the 13x6 has more front-to-back lace space.

What a 13x4 lace wig offers

A 13x4 lace front provides lace across the front of the wig with enough depth for common middle- and side-part looks. Because the lace area is shallower than a 13x6, it can be a practical choice when you usually keep one parting style. Our #613 Platinum Bob, #613 Champagne Body Wave, and 1B/27 Honey Noir Straight use a 13x4 construction.

What a 13x6 lace wig offers

A 13x6 lace front extends farther back at the deepest section. That extra lace space gives more flexibility when shifting a part or creating a deeper-looking parting line. It does not automatically make a wig better: the right option depends on the styles you actually wear and the amount of lace care you want to manage. Compare our 13x6 Signature Straight and 13x6 Cascade Wave.

Where a 5x5 closure fits

A 5x5 closure concentrates the lace around a smaller top section. It offers less ear-to-ear styling space than a lace front, but it may be easier to position and maintain. The 5x5 Bouncy Curl Closure is a dense curly option, while the 5x5 Burgundy Bob Closure combines a short straight shape with a burgundy finish.

How to choose

  • Choose 13x4 if a reliable middle or side part covers most of your styling needs.
  • Choose 13x6 if deeper and more changeable parting space matters to you.
  • Choose 5x5 if you prefer a focused lace area and a simpler everyday routine.

Cap fit, correct placement, careful lace handling and a style that matches your routine matter as much as the lace dimensions. Review the construction listed on each product page before checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Is 13x6 always more natural than 13x4?

No. Both can create a natural-looking front when the cap fits and the lace is positioned carefully. A 13x6 mainly provides more parting depth.

Can a 13x4 lace wig be worn with a side part?

Yes, within the available lace area. How far the part can move depends on the cap construction and placement.
